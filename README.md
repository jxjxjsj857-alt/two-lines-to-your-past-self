A tiny program that writes two lines to your past self: a warning about one specific moment, and one sentence on why that moment mattered. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Two Lines to Your Past Self" bounty, $2 in USDC.

The entry warns against taking a double-pay night shift on 14 October instead of being home — the last night the writer's grandmother was awake enough to know their name — and traces what that choice cost.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
